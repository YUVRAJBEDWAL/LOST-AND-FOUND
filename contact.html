Frequently Asked Questions

How long are lost items kept?

Lost items are typically kept for 90 days. After this period, unclaimed items may be donated or disposed of according to campus policy.

What should I do if I find something valuable?

For valuable items, please bring them directly to the Campus Security Office. For less valuable items, you can report them online and keep them safe until the owner contacts you.

How do I prove ownership of a lost item?

Be prepared to provide specific details about the item, including unique characteristics, when and where it was lost, and any proof of ownership if available.
